01 / THE CHALLENGE

Close the gap between terms and receipts.

Supplier benefits are only useful when contractual terms can be reconciled with receiving activity. Agreements, free-of-charge items, bonuses and credit notes need a common analytical view to identify exceptions and support financial controls.

02 / THE SOLUTION

Turn agreements into analytical checks.

I built a read-only Flask analytics application that compares negotiated bonus and credit-note terms with purchasing receipts and recorded supplier credits. Filtered views show expected benefits, realized benefits, pending reconciliation gaps and supplier-level performance.

06 / TECHNICAL CHALLENGES

Preserve the logic. Isolate the data.

The public application handles supplier attribution, mixed units of measure, agreement boundaries and dual confirmation while preserving additive reconciliation totals. Deterministic XLSX serialization keeps repeated downloads byte-identical. Read-only data access isolates the application from production systems.

08 / DEMO & PRIVACY

Clear boundaries. Responsible sharing.

Synthetic-data demonstration

The anonymous public demo provides read-only dashboards, filters, sortable tables and XLSX exports. Supplier names, item codes and transactions are synthetic; visitors cannot create or edit records.

This portfolio demonstration uses synthetic data and contains no production credentials, patient information, or confidential organizational datasets.
