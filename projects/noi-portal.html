01 / THE CHALLENGE

Close the gap between terms and receipts.

Supplier benefits are only useful when contractual terms can be reconciled with receiving activity. Agreements, free-of-charge items, bonuses and credit notes need a common analytical view to identify exceptions and support financial controls.

02 / THE SOLUTION

Turn agreements into analytical checks.

I built a validation and reporting portal that compares supplier contractual terms, bonuses and credit notes against Goods Received Notes (GRNs). The portal supports non-compliance identification, supplier scorecards, financial controls and S&OP reporting.

06 / TECHNICAL CHALLENGES

Preserve the logic. Isolate the data.

The portfolio version swaps the complete data-access layer while keeping the Flask application unchanged. A compatible cursor interface lets synthetic SQLite data serve the existing application. Parameterized queries address the reviewed filter-query risks.
