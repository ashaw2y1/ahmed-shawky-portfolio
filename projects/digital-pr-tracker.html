01 / THE CHALLENGE

Make the workflow visible.

Purchase requests move through several people and systems. Without a shared view, assignment and purchase-order updates can become difficult to follow, leaving operational teams to reconcile status manually.

02 / THE SOLUTION

A shared view of procurement progress.

I developed a Flask application that captures purchase requests, supports purchaser-assignment workflows, triggers manager notifications and tracks subsequent purchase-order status. The portfolio version retains a local workflow demonstration while disabling external integrations.

06 / TECHNICAL CHALLENGES

Preserve the logic. Isolate the data.

The engineering challenge is demonstrating a connected workflow without depending on live systems or sending real notifications. Configuration isolates external integrations while retaining database-backed application behavior and authentication.
