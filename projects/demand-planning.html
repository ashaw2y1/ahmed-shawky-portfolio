01 / THE CHALLENGE

Planning needs the whole picture.

Replenishment decisions depend on more than stock on hand. Planners need to connect historical consumption, open purchases and receiving activity to understand where inventory is at risk and what to order next.

02 / THE SOLUTION

One connected planning environment.

I built a centralized planning portal that combines consumption, inventory, purchasing and forecast data in one environment. The sanitized demonstration substitutes synthetic records at the extraction boundary, preserving the planning logic and the shape of the incoming data.

06 / TECHNICAL CHALLENGES

Preserve the logic. Isolate the data.

The key demonstration challenge is preserving analytical behavior while replacing every operational extraction. Synthetic inputs retain identical column shapes, allowing Pandas business logic—including classification, trends, reorder calculations and allocation—to run unchanged.
